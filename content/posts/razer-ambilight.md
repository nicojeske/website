Razer Ambilight is a lightweight software to either map your screen to your Razer peripherals or extend your screen to create an "Ambilight" effect.

The tool works for most Razer peripherals that support Chroma like the Razer Ornata Chroma or the Razer Naga Chroma.

## Function

- Maps your screen to your Razer mouse, keyboard and mousepad to create an „ambient effect“
- „true“ Ambilight mode which expands your screen onto your Razer peripherals
- You can change the saturation
- You can change the update rate to reduce the cpu load. Settings can be found by right-clicking the program icon in your notification area on your task bar.
